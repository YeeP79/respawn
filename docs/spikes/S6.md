## Spec

**Question:** Past four players, exactly what steps does a joining player take, and do they work?

**Pass bar:** A repeatable, written procedure that gets a fifth player onto the server, verified by `status` showing five humans. Pass = the procedure works and is short enough to tell a friend once. Fail, or a procedure nobody would follow, sends big-coop back to the drawing board.

**This decides:** Whether big-coop is a viable branch at all, and what we would have to tell players. A ritual nobody will perform is the same as a feature that does not work.

**Why this spike exists:** Sources agree the lobby will not place a fifth player and that they must connect from the developer console, but they disagree on the surrounding cvars — and Left4DHooks turns out to ship `sm_l4dd_unreserve` for exactly this problem, which may change the answer.

## Prior art

Searched 2026-08-28.

- [Playing with more than 4 players — 2024/2025 guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2987986639) — **skimmed**. Says players 1–4 join normally and the 5th onward must connect via console or hit a slot-availability error; mentions `mm_dedicated_force_servers`.
- [How to make a L4D2 server with mods (2026)](https://moddingcommunity.com/blog/how-to-make-a-l4d2-server-with-mods/) — **read in full**. Documents `connect <ip>` and `openserverbrowser` as the two client-side routes, with no special flags.
- Left4DHooks 1.168 — **read in full** — provides `L4D_LobbyUnreserve` and the admin command `sm_l4dd_unreserve`, plus notes L4DToolz "unreserves your server". Lobby *reservation* is very likely the actual mechanism behind the slot error, which means the server may be able to remove the obstacle rather than the player working around it.

**This is the most likely spike to change its own question.** If unreserving removes the ritual entirely, big-coop stops being a branch and becomes a knob — which would collapse two services into one.
