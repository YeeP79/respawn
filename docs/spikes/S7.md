## Spec

**Question:** After adding the base mod stack, how large is the image, and what `ephemeralStorage` value does a task actually need?

**Pass bar:** A measured image size for base-only and base+one-campaign, plus a chosen `ephemeralStorage` figure with stated headroom for the writable layer. Pass = the numbers exist and the chosen value is justified by them rather than rounded up hopefully.

**This decides:** The `ephemeralStorage` config key: its validation range, its default, and whether campaigns force a per-service override.

**Why this spike exists:** `ephemeralStorage` appears **nowhere** in the CDK or the config loader (measured 2026-08-28), while the base image measures **15.6 GB** against Fargate's 20 GiB default. Image layers count against that allocation, leaving roughly 4 GB before a single plugin. Nothing can deploy until this is both sized and wired.
